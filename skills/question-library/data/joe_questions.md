# Joe's probes (from conversation, Oct 2026; his wording, typos fixed)

## 15. Joe, real-time diagnosis
- What's going on, why did that happen?
- Okay, ignore everything else going on right now for just a minute and tell me: is there anything bugging you, or anything I'm not asking you which I should be, or anything you might want to say but don't feel like there's a proper opportunity to, or anything like that? Anything at all?
- What's your understanding of the situation right now, and what was the process that got you from where you started to now? I need to know how you understood everything and how that shaped what happened.
- Guidance: ask rhetorical questions that guide the model toward the right line of reasoning without telling it the answer, so its knowledge deficits show; once the exact missing knowledge is identified, you know what to tell it in future.

## 16. Joe, quick probes

- Did you forget anything?
- Are you sure?
- How do you know that?
- Are you being careful?
- What's the best or most creative or most clever thing you could do here?

## 17. Joe, debrief
- What specific, exact information would have allowed you to complete the task effortlessly and with zero friction?
- What generic, non-specific, or rhetorical questions could have been asked that would have helped you arrive at the correct answer on your own?
- Constraint on that question: it isn't allowed to know anything specific about the circumstance, and it has to be maximally generic, independent of the particular specifics of the situation.

## 18. Joe, troubleshooting method (paraphrased from his description)
- What has already been tried, under what conditions, and what happened?
- Is there existing work online, and what would you search for to find it?
- Practice rules: keep a history log of what was tried, so nothing is re-tested without knowing it.
